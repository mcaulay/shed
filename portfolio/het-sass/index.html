Singularity Sauce Co: Het Sass

Brand strategy  ·  2022

[hero]

The challenge

Singularity Sauce Co made hot sauce in a region where hot sauce wasn't part of everyday life. North East Scotland hadn't traditionally put it on the dinner table, and the business needed a reason for local people to care. The brief was one loose objective with nothing measurable: bring the people of the North East to the party.

The insight

A name local people could own would travel further than another product label. Het Sass was a name I took and made into something real, a stand-alone brand people in the region could feel ownership over. What I hadn't expected was how far it would go. Many North East people work away all over the world, and they took “Aberdeen's hot sauce” with them, which opened doors in England, the Middle East and the Far East.

The strategy

Two phases. The first was making hot sauce relevant locally: one table sauce to sit next to the ketchup and mustard, under a name the region could claim. When another local producer dismissed it as “too parochial”, I knew it had traction. Scarcity helped too. Production was limited, so selling out built demand.

The second, in 2022, was turning Het Sass from a product into a brand, and making it the gateway to Singularity Sauce Co. The Singularity logo came off the front of pack and moved to the back, so people were drawn in by Het Sass and shown the path to Singularity when they wanted to know more. The aim wasn't to reach the customers we already had. It was to give a new audience something to smile about, unencumbered by a brand they didn't know yet.

[strategy01]

The work

Naming, positioning, range architecture, identity, packaging and merchandise. The range grew from one sauce to three (Het Sass, Smokin' Het and Het Het), then to five with Miso and Bacon, each available in three sizes up to a 2,500ml catering pack.

Chris of Dfunkt Robot designed the logo, labels and a suite of merch concepts. We rolled the identity out in stages over a month, with the logo first and then each sauce one by one. Caps, T-shirts, hats, socks, hoodies and gift packs put Het Sass in front of people who might never have heard of it.

The result

By the end of 2022, Het Sass accounted for more than 60% of Singularity Sauce Co's sales. It was the biggest seller at North East markets and events, and was stocked across the UK, including Selfridges, as well as in Germany and France. It was named in Bauce Brothers' Top 100 Sauces in the UK, finished second at the 2023 Bauce Brothers Hot Sauce Awards, and was featured in Hot Sauce: A Fiery Guide to 101 of the World's Best Sauces by Neil Ridley and Dean Honer.

Het Sass was built to smooth the way for Singularity Sauce Co, and it did. People were still asking for it by name long after it was discontinued.

My role

Founder and owner of Singularity Sauce Co. I developed the brand strategy and range, commissioned and directed the identity with Dfunkt Robot, and ran production and sales across the region. I still own the trademark, recipes and assets.
