Jamieson & Carry: The Gentleman's Guide to Valentine's Day

Integrated campaign  ·  2016

[hero]

The challenge

Jamieson & Carry had a highly prestigious reputation in Aberdeen, but one that leaned towards a shrinking, high-earning, upper-class customer base, and its advertising had become stuffy and safe. To grow, it needed to feel approachable to a much wider audience across Aberdeen and Aberdeenshire. Success would be measured on footfall, return on ad spend and how people saw the business.

The idea

Show Jamieson & Carry as available to everyone by putting it in a younger, local context. I persuaded the client to try something new for Valentine's Day and connected them with several other Aberdeen businesses. Together they became The Gentleman's Guide to Valentine's Day.

[detail01]

The work

A booklet distributed through businesses across Aberdeen, supported by local press ads and Facebook advertising. In store, the windows were painted with the campaign graphics and dressed with promotional cards.

[detail02]
[detail03]
[detail04]

The result

The client reported a “significant increase in footfall and sales”. The campaign got Aberdeen talking, and the other businesses involved reported strong results of their own.

My role

Creative Director. I pitched the idea to the client and led the campaign from concept to delivery.
